SignBridge

Helping people who sign and people who speak understand each other

Many people who use sign language struggle to be understood by those who never learned it. SignBridge helps close that gap. Show a sign to your webcam and it appears as words on the screen. One tap, and those words are read out loud.

Signs become words

You can show a sign to the camera and see it written out, so the person in front of you can read it.

Words become speech

The text can be spoken aloud, which helps in places like shops, banks and clinics.

You teach it your signs

It learns from you, so it can match the signs you really use, such as the alphabet or everyday words.

Simple help by voice

Say or type a short request like "hello" or "help". It only does a small, safe list of things.

How to start

  1. Open Sign to speech and turn on the camera.
  2. Show it a sign and record it a few times. Do this for at least two signs.
  3. Press Start recognition and begin signing.
  4. Press Speak text to hear your words.

Will it work on my device?

    Your privacy

    Your camera video stays on your own device. It is never saved and never sent anywhere. The camera and microphone only turn on when you press Start. Your conversation is gone when you close the page.

    Some browsers send your voice to their own service to turn it into text. That is a browser feature, not something SignBridge does.

    What it can't do yet

    SignBridge is an early version. It recognizes only the signs you teach it, using one hand at a time. It does not understand full sign language, which also uses movement, facial expressions and sentence structure.

    Teach gestures

    Turn the camera on, then record each sign at least twice, in separate recordings. Move your hand a little closer and farther each time. Name a sign SPACE to add a space, or DEL to delete a letter.
